---
title: "Web Client"
description: "Chat with your agents from any browser — no app to install."
---

Dash has a web client: a page you open in your browser to chat with your agents.
There's nothing to install. It works anywhere you have a browser and an internet
connection, and it uses the same [remote access](/remote-access) your phone uses to
reach your gateway.

<Note>
The web client is a separate address from Mission Control. Mission Control still
runs on the machine with your agents; the web client is a page hosted elsewhere that
you can open on a laptop, a shared computer, or a phone browser.
</Note>

## Before you start

The web client needs your gateway to already be set up for remote access. If you
haven't done that yet, follow [Remote Access](/remote-access) first — sign in to
Dash from Mission Control, create your gateway, and confirm it shows **Gateway
ready at** followed by your address.

## Sign in and chat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the web client">
    Go to the web client's address (your organization gives you this link — it looks
    something like `https://app.<your relay domain>`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with a passkey">
    Sign in using a passkey — the fingerprint, face, or device unlock your browser
    already offers. There's no password to remember.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your gateway">
    If you have more than one gateway, choose the one you want to chat through.
    The web client remembers your choice on this browser next time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start chatting">
    Open a conversation, or start a new one, and chat with your agent the same way
    you would in Mission Control or the phone app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Conversations you have here are the same ones you see in Mission Control and on
your phone. Anything you send from the web client shows up everywhere else too.
</Tip>

## Manage your devices

Open **Devices** in the web client to see every browser that's signed in and
connected to this gateway. Click **Revoke** next to any browser you no longer
recognize or use — for example, a shared or public computer. Revoking your own
current browser signs it out immediately and sends you back to the gateway picker.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What's happening | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Your gateway is unreachable" | The machine running your gateway is off, asleep, or not connected to the internet | Turn on or wake the machine running Dash and check its internet connection. The web client reconnects automatically once it's back. |
| "This gateway needs to be re-enrolled from Mission Control before web access works" | This gateway was set up for remote access before web support existed, so it hasn't shared what it needs with the web client yet | In Mission Control, open **Settings → Devices → Remote access** and re-run enrollment for this gateway. Then come back and pick it again. |
| "Your web session for this gateway was revoked. Pair again to continue." | This browser's access to the gateway was revoked — either by you from the Devices screen, or by someone else with access to the gateway | You're back at the gateway list. Pick the gateway again to reconnect. If you didn't revoke it yourself, check the Devices screen on your other clients for anything you don't recognize. |
| "No gateways linked to your account yet" | Your Dash account isn't linked to any gateway | In Mission Control, open **Settings → Devices → Remote access** and enroll this machine. |

<Note>
Revoking a browser's access stops it from reaching your agents immediately — if
that browser has a chat open, it disconnects there and then rather than at its
next request. It does not change any other device's access, and your
conversations stay exactly where they are.
</Note>
